* The recurring shape

Four of the six experiments failed silently (~cdc4a22~), and every dimension
below rediscovered the same shape in a new place:

#+begin_quote
Something reports success it never checked, and the symptom surfaces somewhere
else --- usually as an empty transcript.
#+end_quote

- ~8178314~ --- the proxy never started on Linux; because ~tests/test-proxy.sh~
  redirects its stderr, the symptom read as a logging bug.
- ~cdc4a22~ E4 --- a stale proxy held the port, the new ~bind~ died silently, the
  *old* one kept serving and logging.
- ~guile-repl-server.sh:79-81,100~ --- prints ~repl PORT~ and ~proxy ... logging
  to ...~ unconditionally, checking neither return code nor liveness.
- ~guile-repl-eval.sh:44-58~ --- exits 0 for a Scheme error exactly as for a
  value.
- ~Makefile:64-69~ --- ~gmake lint~ cannot fail on Scheme (below).

That is the through-line for prioritisation: the requests that convert a silent
success into a loud failure come first.

* Verified in this pass

One claim was cheap enough to settle rather than repeat, so it was:

#+begin_src sh
printf '\n(this-is-not-balanced (\n' >> skills/guile-repl-proxy/scripts/guile-repl-proxy.scm
gmake lint; echo $?
#+end_src

#+begin_example

#+end_example

'warning|error' || true~, so the target exits 0 and CI's "Shell and Scheme lint"
step (~checks.yml:59~) is *decorative for Scheme*. This is the exact failure
class the repository was built to catch, inside the repository's own checks. The
working tree was restored after the probe.

A second one settled itself. Checking for orphans after ~gmake checks~ found
*eleven* proxies still bound on nexus, owned by ~jwalsh~ --- another login, another
checkout --- the oldest running ~1-00:58~, all of them still invoking
~./bin/guile-repl-proxy.scm~, the path deleted in ~8d71a62~. They predate that
refactor and have held ports through it.

Worse than the record says. *Nine of the eleven proxy to a dead REPL*: the
children were ~-c '(sleep 86400)'~ and exited at 24 hours, while the proxies did
not. So the listener survives the thing it proxies, and a client gets the
repository's signature failure:

#+begin_src sh
printf '(+ 1 1)\n' | nc -N 127.0.0.1 42042; echo $?
#+end_src

#+begin_example
0
#+end_example

An empty reply and a *successful* exit status --- the same shape as E4, from the
other end. That makes A4 a measured need rather than a tidiness request, and adds

* 2. Trace, transcript and what an agent can observe

** Already closed

Block buffering, because ~get-bytevector-some~ on an unbuffered socket returns
one byte (E2, ~guile-repl-proxy.scm:118-120~); per-direction EOF retirement plus
~shutdown peer 1~, because ~nc -N~'s half-close was tearing down the whole
connection and losing every reply (E3, ~:108-140~); rotation at 4 MiB × 5, at
startup and *between* connections so no session is cut mid-transcript (E5,
~:66-90,206-207~); non-UTF-8 chunks rendered as ~#<N non-utf8 bytes>~ with the
bytes still forwarded (~:92-96~); ~~3,'0d~ needs ~(ice-9 format)~, which
~simple-format~ accepted silently (E8, ~:32-34~). Transcripts live under
~${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_DATA}/projects/<slug>/~ and never in-repo, with
~.gitignore:9-15~ as "belt and braces in case someone points ~--log~ at the
working directory" --- a REPL transcript contains whatever was evaluated.

Worth keeping in view: ~b9600a6~'s note declined to scrub the already-generated
workspace transcripts, on the grounds that rewriting a record falsifies
evidence.

** Open gaps

- *A failed evaluation is indistinguishable from a value.*
  ~guile-repl-eval.sh:44-58~ exits 0 either way, and the banner/prompt stripper
  (~:63-69~) can swallow the error text outright.
- *No session or eval id.* ~;; connection N~ restarts at 1 on every proxy restart
  (~guile-repl-proxy.scm:185~) into an append-only file, so two ~connection 1~
  blocks coexist. Correlation is by wall clock --- and the millisecond lines are
  localtime (~:47~) while the session header is UTC (~:38-39~).
- *No timing record.* Latency is recoverable only by subtracting timestamps by
  hand; no per-form duration, no ~,time~ capture.
- *~--status~ lies by omission.* It prints the *configured* log path
  (~guile-repl-server.sh:55~), not the running proxy's actual ~--log~, no size or
  mtime, and never mentions ~repl.stderr~/~proxy.stderr~ --- while the proxy's
  own eval #2 asks an agent to check exactly that.
- *stderr is unrotated and asymmetric*; rotation covers only ~repl.log~.
- *Rotation is silent and lossy*: no "rotated from" marker in the new file,
  generation 5 is ~delete-file~'d (~:86-87~), and because rotation only happens
  between connections a single long Geiser session grows unbounded.
- *Idle close is invisible client-side*: the 300 s ~select~ timeout logs ~!! idle
  for 300s, closing~ (~:123-127~) and drops the connection; the client sees only a
  closed socket.
- *No redaction or excerpt tooling*, though proxy eval 3 requires producing a
  reviewed excerpt rather than committing the transcript.
- No ~gmake tail~/~log~ target (~Makefile:15-24~).
- ~,locals~ reports no variables at a frame where an argument is in scope ---
  unexplained, carried from ~guile-sicp~, and tagged *not established*
  (~rationale.org:96~). It stays a question, not a request.

* 4. Evals and the test bed

** What the bed actually is

Three layers, two of them automated:

1. *Deterministic regressions* --- ~tests/test-proxy.sh~, seven checks, one per
   EXPERIMENTS.org failure (E1, E2, E3, E3b, E4, E5, E6). Gate run 36463634928
   printed ~7 passed, 0 failed~.
2. *Eval-suite structure* --- ~tests/validate-evals.py~ (~a3c7413~) checks
   ~skills/*/evals/evals.json~ against the skill-creator schema: allowed keys,
   ids consecutive from 1, ~skill_name~ matching SKILL.md frontmatter, 3--4 evals
   per skill, and a ~VAGUE~ regex (~:24~) rejecting untestable expectations such
   as "helpful" or "as expected". Its note records that it catches all seven
   injected faults. 12 evals, 63 expectations, three skills (~c06d9e7~).
3. *One measured benchmark* --- skill-creator ~with_skill~ vs ~without_skill~,
   committed as evidence (~fd00b82~, ~e29e9a3~), at ~runs_per_configuration: 1~:

| skill             | pass with | pass without |    Δ pass |  Δ time |  Δ tokens |
|-------------------+-----------+--------------+-----------+---------+-----------|
| guile-repl-eval   |      0.95 |         0.85 |     +0.10 |  −5.5 s |     −3237 |
| guile-repl-proxy  |    0.8167 |       0.7665 |     +0.05 | −132.2 s |    −7096 |
| guile-repl-server |    0.9583 |       0.9167 |     +0.04 | −153.5 s |     −110 |

The honest part is already written down in ~benchmark.json:notes~: *five of
twelve cases are explicit ties* --- ~reload-after-edit~, ~lint-proxy-script~,
~transcript-of-eval~, ~identify-runtime~, ~start-and-trace-fib~,

* The test bed

Two fixtures, because one cannot show both shapes:

  redefined to exercise reload-after-edit, a file that produces ~guild compile

The matrix:

| axis    | values                                                                                     |
|---------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| host    | nexus (FreeBSD 15.1, ~guile3~ 3.0.10, bare ~guile~ 2.2.7); ubuntu-latest (~guile-3.0~); macOS (Homebrew ~guile~) |

Deterministic layers --- ~test-proxy~, ~check-evals~, ~calibrate~, ~ship-check~,

One row per ~(host, guile, skill, case, arm, run)~: pass per expectation,
~pass_rate~, ~discrimination = pass_with − pass_without~, ~latency_s~,
~tokens_in/out~, ~cost_usd~, ~tool_calls~ (currently always 0 --- fix),
~skill_fired~ for precision/recall, ~calibrated~, ~host~, ~guile_version~,

N ≥ 3, per-skill trigger precision/recall, and a verdict column for the corpus.

Plumbing: new targets ~calibrate~, ~corpus~, ~try-in-fixture~, ~bench~. The
first and third join ~checks~, since both are deterministic and need no model,
keeping ~checks~ the single command it is today (~Makefile:106~,
~checks.yml:73-77~). ~bench~ stays out of CI for the reason ~checks.yml:7-13~
already gives, but gains a committed ~benchmark-baseline.json~ so ~gmake bench
--check~ can fail on a discrimination regression --- the one thing a one-shot

* Evals backlog

Each of these fails today, which is the point --- an eval that cannot fail is
decoration (~PLAN.org:44~).

- *error-vs-value* --- ask for a value from a form that throws; fail if the
  answer reports success. Measures B1.
- *correlate a failure to its transcript lines* --- impossible today without an
  eval id (B1/B4).
- *lint-exit-status* --- given a ~.scm~ that will not compile, does the agent
  report ~gmake lint~ as passing? It would. Discriminates only after C1.
- *cold start on a 2.2.7-only host* --- the *start decision*, not the version
  report; ~evals/right-guile-binary~ covers only the report.
- *cold start with no guile at all* --- does the agent notice the success message
  was a lie? Directly tests A1.
- *second concurrent session in one cwd* --- identical derived port: does it find
  the override or kill the other session's REPL?
- *orphan started without ~--debug~* while ~--status~ says "listening" --- harder
  than ~evals/stale-listener~.
- *proxy outlives its REPL* --- connect to a proxy whose target is dead: the
  connection succeeds, the reply is empty, ~nc~ exits 0. Does the agent diagnose a
  dead target, or report that the form evaluated to nothing? Measured live today;
  no eval covers it.
- *no-src-dir* --- modules at the root: does the agent notice load path ~none~,
  or claim the module loaded?
- *stale-generated-doc* --- ~README.org~ edited, ~README.md~ not regenerated:
  ~gmake readme~, or a claim that the docs are consistent?
- *rotation amnesia* --- plant a rotation between two evals and ask for the
  earlier one; pass only if ~repl.log.1~ is read.
- *idle-close attribution* --- hold past 300 s; pass only if the ~!!~ line is
  cited rather than a crash hypothesised.
- *stderr-first* --- break the interpreter name (~8178314~'s scenario); pass only
  if ~proxy.stderr~ is read before the proxy source.
- *redaction* --- stronger than the current eval 3: require a *produced*, masked
  excerpt, not just a refusal.
- *host-fact reporting* --- does any answer about "the Guile version" name the
  node it was measured on? E9's lesson, covered by nothing.
- *absent ~gh skill~* --- is the publish step reported as SKIPPED, or as a
  release?
- *version-bump judgement* --- given a flag added to a shipped script, is minor
  chosen per RELEASING.org, and bumped before pushing?

A test bed for these needs a fake REPL that emits known errors, non-UTF-8 bytes
and slow replies; a planted stale proxy; a clock injector for deterministic
timestamps; and per-case ~timing.json~, a pattern the workspace artifacts already
established.

Ranked by the criterion at the top --- convert a silent success into a loud
failure, cheaply:

6. *A7* --- the ~./bin/~ hint an agent reads immediately after a successful start.
7. *D1* --- a fixture project, which unblocks D2, C5, C6 and most of the eval
   backlog.
8. *D4* --- no committed result without its host, so the next E9 cannot happen.
9. *D2* + *D3* --- calibration and repeat runs, at which point the tie/negative
   findings can be settled instead of quoted.

*A4* joins that list at position 4 on the strength of today's measurement: an
orphan that answers a connection and returns nothing is the failure this
repository is named after. B3, B5--B7, C5--C8, D5--D8 follow; D7 and D8 are the
two L items and should wait for the fixture and the calibration harness.
